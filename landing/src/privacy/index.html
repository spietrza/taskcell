Privacy policy

Taskcell runs on your machine or your server and sends us nothing. This page covers the little that reaches us: visits to taskcell.dev, payments and email.

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Code Precision sp. z o.o., osiedle Piastowskie 109/14, 61-154 Poznań, Poland. For anything about your data, write to support@taskcell.dev.

The Taskcell software

Taskcell has no telemetry. Your code, issues, keys, run logs and usage stay on the machine or server where you run it and are not sent to us. The model providers and code hosts you connect (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, GitHub, GitLab or Linear) receive data from your installation under their own terms; we are not a party to that.

Visiting taskcell.dev

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts and scripts are served from taskcell.dev itself.

The site is served by Cloudflare. To deliver and protect it, Cloudflare processes each request: your IP address, browser, the page asked for and the time. Our own request logs on Cloudflare, used to find errors, are kept for up to 7 days. The country Cloudflare derives from your IP address picks the currency and the language of the page; we do not store it.

Your choice of theme and language is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

The hosting request form is a placeholder and sends nothing.

Payments

Payments go through Stripe Checkout. Stripe collects your card details, name, billing address and, if you give one, your tax ID; card details never reach us. For each order we keep the Stripe session and customer IDs, your email address, the plan, the amounts, the currency, the status and the date.

We process this to provide what you bought (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to meet accounting and tax duties (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). Order records are kept for 5 years from the end of the year of payment, as Polish tax law requires.

Email

When you write to support@, security@ or billing@taskcell.dev, we get your address and your message. We use them to answer you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in replying) and keep the conversation for as long as the matter is open and up to 2 years after, unless it is part of an order record.

Who else handles your data

  • Cloudflare: hosting of taskcell.dev and the order records.
  • Stripe: payments, invoices and receipts.
  • Zoho: our mailboxes.

Some of them process data outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Those transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

If you report a security issue through GitHub, GitHub's own privacy statement applies.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, a copy of it, its correction or deletion, a limit on its use, or object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Write to support@taskcell.dev; we answer within a month. You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl).

Changes

When this policy changes, this page changes and the date at the top moves.
