Hosted plans

A hosted plan (Solo, Team or Pro) is a dedicated server where we run Taskcell for you, back it up daily and keep it updated. The plans differ in how many runs can go at once and how many people can use them, as listed on the pricing section. The models still run on your own keys, and we add no margin to what they cost.

You must not use a hosted plan to break the law, attack other systems, or run work you have no right to run on the code you connect. If you do, we may suspend the plan after telling you why.

We aim to keep hosted servers available at all times, but maintenance and failures can interrupt them; we announce planned maintenance in advance by email. When a plan ends, we keep your server and its data for 30 days so you can export them, then delete them.

Prices and payment

Prices are on taskcell.dev and in the checkout. They include VAT where it applies. The currency follows your country: złoty in Poland, euro in the rest of Europe, US dollars elsewhere. Payments are handled by Stripe; the contract is made when the checkout confirms your payment, and Stripe emails the receipt or invoice.

Subscriptions

Solo, Team and Pro are paid monthly or yearly, in advance, and renew automatically at the end of each period. You can cancel at any time by writing to billing@taskcell.dev; the plan then runs until the end of the period you paid for and does not renew. Apart from the cases below, we do not refund the rest of a period.

The 7-day pass

The 7-day Solo pass is a one-time payment for 7 days of a Solo server. It does not renew. If you then subscribe from the link we give you, the amount you paid for the pass is credited toward your first subscription invoice.
